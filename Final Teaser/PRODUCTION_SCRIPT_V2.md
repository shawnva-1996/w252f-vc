## 3. Scene direction (what it should feel like)

### Opening — 0 to 11 seconds
The first emotion must be **affection, not sadness**. We meet Monica through her joyful Christmas greeting to family. Her phone becomes the bridge to finding the gathering. Cut on her positive curiosity to the **temporary event-ad concept**. No tears or melodramatic longing.

### Invitations — 11 to 21 seconds
Jessa is warm and inviting; Ate Susie is the energy lift. Her "Besh!" is a lively barkada moment, not scolding. Move visually from intimate conversation to shared community excitement. P05 uses a happy held expression from Ate Susie's **already available P04 source**. The transition effect (gentle zoom/brief white-gold flash) is made **entirely in post-production**, not generated by Flow.

### Dance montage — 21 to 32 seconds
Show this as **past Christmas highlights**, explicitly archival. Favor recognizable smiling faces and stage energy. Cut on musical beats across three source selections. The existing archive may show only some of the desired group: DO NOT claim every named dancer is visible without verification. The twelve intended featured dancers are **Leizel, Susie, Margie, Flor, Riza, Anavee, Lilia, Usha, Iad, Willy, Adrian, Donna**. If final release requires each to appear, ask for verified individual moments/photos; otherwise treat montage as a general joyful historic cutaway.

### Joining + rules — 32 to 47 seconds
Come back to Jessa and Monica to give story continuity. Jessa's outfit is the same across P03/P09/P11. Monica says yes. Make sure the P11 phone has its front-facing display clearly visible. The supplied rules image appears as a separate full-screen insert in V2; do not replace it with fake or paraphrased rules.

### Final call to join — 47 to 58 seconds
Mommy Tere's line should feel like a wholehearted invitation. René delivers the quick English closing line. Land on the temporary end-card information with enough time to register the date and venue; replace it with actual official art when supplied.

**Music:** Keep the simple original instrumental bed already used in POC V2, unless the director supplies an approved replacement. Avoid unlicensed popular Christmas remixes. Increase musical energy during 11–32s, duck music for speech, then let the ending rise. Mute the historical dance archive's original audio unless specifically cleared.

**Dialogue:** Keep natural Tagalog/Taglish and the actual voice audio from the correct Flow performance. Most crucial audio QA: Monica P01 complete "na kayo", Ate Susie's P04 complete phrase, Jessa P09 (verify actual variant), Monica P12 full "'yan!", plus Tere and René. If a current POC edit trims the final word, adjust source in/out points rather than inventing new dialogue. Do not turn scripted words into claimed recordings.

**Subtitles:** Add accurate subtitles from what each person actually says; one clear line or at most two short lines. Use bold high-contrast text with a subtle shadow, avoid covering faces/phones. Subtitle timing should follow spoken syllables, not the start/end of the whole scene.

**Motion:** Use one consistent Christmas visual language—warm golden light, red/green holiday highlights, friendly gentle camera motion. No aggressive glitch transitions. One strong light-flash at P05 → P06 is enough.

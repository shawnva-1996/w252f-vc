## 3. Scene direction (what it should feel like)

### Opening — 0 to 11 seconds
The first emotion must be **affection, not sadness**. We meet Monica through her joyful Christmas greeting to family. Her phone becomes the bridge to finding the gathering. Cut on her positive curiosity to the **real invitation poster** (or the filmed iPhone showing it). No tears or melodramatic longing.

### Invitations — 11 to 21 seconds
Jessa is warm and inviting; Ate Susie is the energy lift. Her "Mga Pangga!" is a warm, encouraging barkada moment, not scolding. Move visually from intimate conversation to shared community excitement. P05 uses a happy held expression from Ate Susie's **already available P04 source**. The transition effect (gentle zoom/brief white-gold flash) is made **entirely in post-production**, not generated by Flow.

### Dance montage — 21 to 32 seconds
Show this as **past Christmas highlights**, explicitly archival. Favor recognizable smiling faces and stage energy. Cut on musical beats across three source selections. The existing archive may show only some of the desired group: DO NOT claim every named dancer is visible without verification. The twelve intended featured dancers are **Leizel, Susie, Margie, Flor, Riza, Anavee, Lilia, Usha, Iad, Willy, Adrian, Donna**. If final release requires each to appear, ask for verified individual moments/photos; otherwise treat montage as a general joyful historic cutaway.

### Joining + rules — 32 to 47 seconds
Come back to Ate Margie and Monica to give story continuity. P03 is Jessa's only shot in V3. Monica says yes. Make sure the P11 phone has its front-facing display clearly visible, showing the program poster. ### Final call to join — 47 to 58 seconds
Mommy Tere's line should feel like a wholehearted invitation. Kuya Rene, Ate Ana and Ate Joy deliver the quick English closing line together in one group shot. Land on the temporary end-card information with enough time to register the date and venue; replace it with actual official art when supplied.

It may be played even if the final stage order changes. Do not manufacture an event order or depict footage as live if it is archival.

- **00:15–00:30 — FAITH & TOGETHERNESS:** genuine praise/worship, prayer/carol/candlelight moments IF they have happened before cutoff; respectful audience shots.
- **00:30–01:05 — THE SHOW:** for EACH contest group, give similar-duration high-energy highlights; include stage wide, striking coordinated dance move, reaction; do not overfocus on one group.
- **01:05–01:20 — JUDGES & SUSPENSE:** audience cheering, judges reacting, glimpses of every group, music rising. No scores, rankings, secretly filmed papers, or invented reactions.

If worship has not occurred by editing cutoff, shift that ~15-second segment to authentic fellowship/interactions; do NOT insert fabricated worship footage.

Confirm exact MC cue, background music behavior, and AV handover. The last frame should not disclose winning group or suggest judges favor anyone.

## 3. One-person camera behavior
- **Primary projection format:** Shoot LANDSCAPE 16:9, 1080p/30fps, H.264 if selectable; lock orientation. If actual stage screen requires different ratio, confirm before filming. Promotional Final Teaser 9:16 is a separate asset. Center key subjects enough to allow later portrait crop.
- Set lens to **1x** for most stage shots; avoid switching to unstable zoom, digital zoom, or 4K/60 unless required. Avoid auto exposure changing wildly across stage lights; test on site, keep faces visible.
- For stage dances: pre-position to see full formations; never block guests, walk into stage path, or obstruct judges.
- Hold camera still through full movement and through the crowd reaction. Capture 2–3 standalone reaction clips separate from each team's routine.
- Capture 5–10s of applause/cheering room tone for editing, and one clean directed "Merry Christmas" if consent obtained. Avoid dubbing invented dialogue.
- Use consistent simple clip labels if filenames can be changed; otherwise store by chronological camera file order. Write down team names and number of groups from organizers — do not guess.

## 4. Production schedule: reverse-plan from awards playback, not event finish
Assume **T = SDE playback immediately before prize announcements**, not necessarily 5 PM.

- **T–90 to T–60 min:** All required event segments should ideally have been captured. Use transfers in batches, not one enormous end-of-day copy; monitor upload/copy success.
- **T–60 min:** HARD CUT-OFF for new footage in the primary SDE. Last performance must be available, otherwise prepare a shorter version without misrepresenting missing teams, or move playback later.
- **T–60 to T–45:** Claude indexes media, creates/selects proxies or trim in/out, constructs timeline from prebuilt template.
- **T–45 to T–20:** Make first cut, equalize performance coverage, check pacing and caption accuracy. Use preset effects and prelicensed soundtrack.
- **T–20 to T–10:** Render H.264 1080p final (fallback 720p if necessary); check opening, all teams, audio, endframe, no accidental winner reveal. Render speed depends on the available machine; durations are planning budgets, not guarantees.
- **T–10 to T–0:** Copy locally onto presentation laptop AND USB backup; AV operator opens/plays file and tests screen ratio, loudness and smooth playback; cue MC.
- **If last stage performance ends fewer than 60 min before awards:** Simultaneously filming AND manually editing with one person is a real bottleneck. Prebuild nearly everything, use a second person to import/select or shift prize presentation later. Do not claim a guaranteed 60-min edit without a rehearsal.

## 6. Strict editorial and logistical guardrails
- Equal/fair representation for each competing group (same approximate screen time, ideally 4–6 seconds per team, adjusted for number of teams and total runtime); mix order or use live performance order but **do not imply a score**.
- Never include final results, actual winner name, private scorecards or predicted winners; film **before** prize ceremony, screen **before** announcement.
- Do not claim worship footage exists if worship is scheduled later.
- Other planned items (food fellowship, carols, candlelight, gifts, raffle) are from the poster, but exact order remains organizer-dependent. Prioritize filmable moments that actually occur before the cutoff.
- Use footage from THIS event for this SDE. Any supplied historical inserts must be labeled clearly and approved; preferably avoid archive altogether.
- Confirm participant filming/publication permissions, particularly children or sensitive moments, and obtain organizer signoff before external/social sharing.
- Have the MC/AV operator know the exact moment the music stops and screen cuts black.
- Human QA is mandatory even if Claude automated the edit: inspect all team representation and projection audio/video.

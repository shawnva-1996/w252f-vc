## Creative direction for actors, sound and rhythm
- **First 34s:** comedy is driven by actual Flow visual footage; Kuya's reactions are affectionate and friendly; don't insult anyone or invent a personal backstory. SFX are timed to motion. Vertical 9:16 framing throughout.
- **34–64s:** each of the ten original celebrant photographs gets a **full three-second main-stage reveal**, never a tiny fleeting thumbnail. Dynamic red/gold/green cards, group colors and the 2/10 to 10/10 progress bar are done in Remotion, not Flow.
- **64–84s:** slow down just enough to hear real feelings. No fake family VO from Gemini; only actual voice recordings. If relatives submit long messages, make an optional extended personal-video cut separately; 16 seconds cannot carry lengthy tributes to all ten.
- **84–90s:** happy communal landing, not a mournful fadeout.
- **Sound:** Google **Gemini TTS** for the narrator; preserve clean genuine voices. Original/licensed fun Christmas track for first two-thirds; smooth tonal transition into warm instrumental; approved party sting at end. The user's requested PBB **“Pinoy Ako”** remains **uncleared** until permission is obtained.
- **Technical:** four source clips verified as **360×640 at 24 fps, ~10.03s**, H.264 video with AAC audio streams. The 1080×1920 master requires scaling (source is not HD). Sound content was **not** audited from the MP4s; mute/rebuild as necessary.

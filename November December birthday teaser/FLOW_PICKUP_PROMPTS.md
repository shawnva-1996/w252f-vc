## How to use (same pattern as W252F-VC Teaser 3)
1. In a new **Google Flow Agent** session, supply **[GOOGLE_FLOW_AGENT_INSTRUCTION_POC.md](GOOGLE_FLOW_AGENT_INSTRUCTION_POC.md)** as **Agent Instruction**.
2. Supply **[GOOGLE_FLOW_AGENT_PROMPT_POC.md](GOOGLE_FLOW_AGENT_PROMPT_POC.md)** as **first Agent Prompt** (episode context).
3. For each Flow pickup, attach the specified **approved original reference photograph** where the member consents and Flow permits this mode (P05A uses Ate Joy photo 2) and copy **only the text inside that pickup's `text` code block** below into the Flow VIDEO generator. The editor/agent handles references, costs, timing, QA and audio.
4. For graphics-only and real-footage pickups below **there is intentionally NO Flow video prompt**; Remotion/FFmpeg assembles them without spending credits. Do not generate unrelated AI video merely to fill a numbered pickup.
5. **Preset for all Flow pickups:** **Omni Flash 1.2, 360p, 9:16, 10s source, one output**. Confirm model/options and actual debit in user's Flow interface before each paid operation. User-reported budget **50 credits/account, ~8 accounts**, current expected 10 credits per 10s, accounts separate; track actual credits. Do not bypass usage/content restrictions or automatically retry.
6. After an accepted **same-person/same-environment** PxxA, **Save frame** on the last clean frame; for an approved PxxB, use **Extend** where eligible or **Frames to Video** with the saved frame as the start image, plus the original identity photo where supported. For named celebrants, approved original photos may be used as visual references in Flow where the service permits this; original photos and genuine footage remain available for post-production. Each different person begins with their own approved original image; **never another person's last frame**.
7. Kuya is **never a visual character**, and all narration is produced **separately using Gemini Filipino TTS**. Do not have Flow speak lines, print titles, add subtitles, or insert copyrighted soundtrack. Music must be original/cleared rather than PBB recordings unless rights obtained.
8. Upload the generated MP4(s) and saved last-frame PNG(s) to user-approved storage with the pickup filenames and actual credit charges. No result from any of these prompts has yet been claimed rendered.

### P01 — BIRTHDAY EMERGENCY (optional Flow, final 00–04s)

**Priority:** low; use Remotion first, as one source 10s clip is excessive for four final seconds. **Reference:** none; no human faces. **Narration added later:** “Attention, housemates! Birthday emergency!”

```text
Create a lively cinematic vertical 9:16 close-up of a ridiculously tall stack of wrapped Christmas presents in a warm, festive Filipino Christmas party room. The gift stack trembles, a big red ribbon flutters past the camera, and colorful fairy lights sparkle in the background. Make the action fast, funny, harmless and joyful, suitable as a birthday comedy cold open. Keep it a single continuous shot with a smooth playful camera movement. No people, dialogue, subtitles, signs, logos or on-screen text.
```

**Post:** Three fast visual cuts of the single shot can create the first-three-second impact. Alarm/SFX/title all added in editor. Do not insert PBB TV logo or original PBB music.

## Audio mix and pacing
- Audio-only narrator: Google **Gemini TTS** in Filipino/Taglish, one consistent adult male preset (test Gacrux mature, Charon informative, Orus firm); voice direction confident, warm, comedic, not an imitation of real PBB narration.
- Give important dialogue its full natural duration; exact text is provisional until timing is tested. If 5s lines are too long, trim words in script **before** audio generation; don't time-stretch unnaturally or clip syllables.
- PBB “Pinoy Ako” is a **music reference, not cleared audio**. Use original/cleared music for the prototype; if official recording use is desired later, obtain appropriate authorization for the intended screening/posting.
- Entry/exit beat: musical stings under punchlines, then dramatic dip into real family messages, ending festive. Duck soundtrack behind all spoken audio.
- No generated subtitles/titles in Flow; Remotion owns all names/date text for spellcheck and exact placement.

## Camera and identity continuity
- Film style: glossy Filipino Christmas special, energetic stage-light warmth, red/gold/green with festive practical lighting, playful reactions; not a direct PBB set/logo recreation.
- For identifiable members, original approved photo is **identity authority** each time; do not use an AI output as substitute reference.
- Fewer Flow generations, more strong editing. Not everyone needs to talk or be animated; every person **must** appear by original portrait at least once.
- One named person per real-person Flow shot; do not ask Flow to fabricate multi-person crowds or new family faces.
